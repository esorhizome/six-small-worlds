# Six Small Worlds — the short-form mini-course

*Format: TikTok / Shorts / Reels · 24 clips · 15–60 s each · vertical ·
every clip stands alone; together they're a ladder into the long course.*

## The format contract

Each world gets **4 clips**, always in the same roles:

1. **Hook** — the wow, no teaching. The pattern appears; one line of awe.
2. **Build** — a 60-second make-along. Screen-recorded code, big font,
   3–5 text-on-screen steps. Ends running.
3. **Twist** — change ONE number, get a new world. The replayable clip;
   invite duets/stitches ("what did you get?").
4. **Bridge** — "this is the actual mechanic in [game]" + point to the long
   course. The only clip allowed a CTA.

Rules of the room: one idea per clip · text lines ≤ 6 words · the visual is
the star, faces optional · captions always on · never punish the viewer for
not knowing something ("no maths degree needed" is a recurring on-screen line).

## Cadence

2 clips/week ≈ 12 weeks of material; 3/week ≈ 8 weeks. Hooks and Twists are
cheap (screen capture + captions); Builds need one rehearsed take; batch all
four of a world in one session right after recording that world's long-form
episodes — same project files, same energy.

## Sound & compliance notes

- The studio's procedural audio IS the soundtrack — every game generates its
  own sound, which doubles as a flex. No licensed-music worries.
- Loop-close every Hook/Twist (pattern completes as the clip restarts).
- Alt-text and on-screen captions per platform accessibility norms; the
  reduce-motion ethos of the games applies to strobing edits too.
