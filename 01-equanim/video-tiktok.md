# World 1 · equanim — short-form shot lists (4 clips)

*Format per STYLE.md and the [short-form course map](../00-course/shortform-tiktok.md):
four clips — Hook, Build, Twist, Bridge — vertical, 15–60 s, one idea each.
Ladder positions: Hook is clip #2, Build #5, Twist #10, Bridge #20.*

House notes for all four: captions on, always. Text-on-screen lines are six
words or fewer. Every Hook and Twist loop-closes (the pattern completes as
the clip restarts). No strobing edits — the games' reduce-motion ethos
applies to the cutting room too. Per the course map, the Bridge is the only
clip that points anywhere off-platform.

**Production once, reuse four times:** set the Godot window to 1080×1920
(Project Settings → Display → Window) and record the starter scenes directly.
The morph in clips 1 and 2 uses `starter/superformula.gd` plus the lerp
variation from lesson 2's go-deeper. Big editor font for any code on screen.

---

## Clip 1 · Hook — the wow

- **Hook line (spoken + on screen):** "This is one equation."
- **Length:** 18 s.
- **Idea (one only):** a single outline keeps becoming new named things.

| Seconds | Shot | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | White outline on dark: a circle, idling in a slow turn. | "this is one equation" |
| 3–7 | The circle pinches into a five-armed starfish. No cut — one continuous morph. | "still one equation" |
| 7–11 | Starfish relaxes into a square with polite corners, then an eight-petal daisy. | "no maths degree needed" |
| 11–15 | Quick gentle run: diamond → nineteen-armed sun → back towards circle. | "one equation. six dials." |
| 15–18 | It settles as the opening circle, mid-turn — frame-matched to second 0. | *(no text — let it loop)* |

- **Caption:** One equation drawing a circle, a starfish, a square and a
  daisy. It's called the superformula (Gielis, 2003). Sound on for the hum.
- **Hashtags:** #maths #generativeart #creativecoding #oddlysatisfying
- **CTA:** none — the loop is the ask.
- **Loop-close:** last frame = first frame, mid-turn, so the restart is
  invisible.
- **Alt-text:** A white line drawing on a dark background morphs smoothly
  between a circle, a starfish, a square, and a flower.

## Clip 2 · Build — the 60-second make-along

- **Hook line (spoken):** "Draw every flower with one formula."
- **Length:** 60 s. Screen-recorded code, big font. Ends running.
- **Idea (one only):** from empty editor to a daisy on screen, for real.

| Seconds | Shot | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | The finished daisy, turning. Then hard cut to an empty Godot editor. | "draw every flower. one formula." |
| 4–12 | Project manager → 2D Scene → node appears. | "step 1 — new 2D scene" |
| 12–20 | Right-click node → Attach Script → empty script. | "step 2 — one node, one script" |
| 20–38 | The superformula function pasted, then held still; the three lines pointed at with the cursor, unhurried. | "step 3 — paste the formula" |
| 38–48 | The six dial values typed at the top: m 8, sharpness 1-3-3, stretches 1. | "step 4 — m=8, press play" |
| 48–60 | F6. The daisy appears and idles in its slow turn. Hold. No cut until the end. | "a daisy. you made this." |

- **Caption:** Sixty seconds, one node, one formula — the superformula
  (Gielis, 2003). Full written lesson is free, and it goes much gentler than
  sixty seconds.
- **Hashtags:** #godot #creativecoding #learntocode #mathart
- **CTA:** none — it ends running, which is the point.
- **Alt-text:** A screen recording of the Godot editor: a short script is
  pasted, and a flower-shaped white outline appears and slowly turns.

## Clip 3 · Twist — change one number

- **Hook line (spoken + on screen):** "m=5 is a starfish. m=19 is ???"
- **Length:** 25 s.
- **Idea (one only):** one dial, m, decides what the shape *is*.

| Seconds | Shot | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | The starfish card, turning. The Inspector slider `symmetry_m` visible beside it, set to 5. | "m=5 — a starfish" |
| 4–8 | Slider dragged to 7, live. The shape gains two arms mid-turn. | "m=7 — ???" |
| 8–13 | Slider to 12: a soft gear. To 3: a rounded triangle. Each held a beat. | "one number changed" |
| 13–19 | Slider glides to 19: a spiky sun that barely fits the card. Hold. | "m=19" |
| 19–25 | Glide back down to 5; the starfish reassembles exactly as at second 0. | "what did you get?" |

- **Caption:** The m dial counts the arms. Five is a starfish. Nineteen is —
  honestly, you tell me. Duet or stitch this with your m; name it something
  good.
- **Hashtags:** #maths #generativeart #godot #creativecoding #mathart
- **CTA:** comment or duet with your m and its name — that's the whole game.
- **Loop-close:** the return to m=5 lands frame-matched to the opening.
- **Alt-text:** A star-shaped outline gains and loses arms as a slider
  labelled symmetry_m is dragged between 3 and 19.
